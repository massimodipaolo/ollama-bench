# When will I get my refund?

*IRS logo*

If you e-file, you can generally expect your refund **within 10 - 21 days**. The projected refund date "Where's My Refund" provides may change as your tax return moves through processing. A date change is not a sign of a problem. Keep in mind that IRS telephone assistors will not be able to provide additional information.

Please **do not call our toll-free number** unless "Where's My Refund" indicates that you should.

## 1. When to check...

- 72 hours after you e-filed
- 4 weeks after you mailed your paper return

## 2. What you need...

- Social Security Number
- Filing status
- Exact refund amount

- Go to... *Where's My Refund? logo*
